FAQ

Questions, answered

What does the free trial include?

Full access to any one bot of your choice for 24 hours: it runs, finds leads and drafts emails exactly like a paid plan. You can use the trial once per account.

Which email address do the emails come from?

Your own. You connect your mailbox (any provider with SMTP, such as Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or Hostinger) in Settings, so replies come straight to you and your sending reputation stays yours.

Is cold email legal?

B2B cold email is lawful in many countries when it is honest, identifies the sender, includes a postal address and offers a working opt-out. The bots enforce all of this and only email countries where it is allowed; the rest are kept for manual outreach. You remain responsible for how you use it.

Can I subscribe to more than one bot?

Yes. Each bot is its own subscription, so you add exactly the ones that match the services you sell. Renewing early adds time on top of what you have left.

How do I pay?

Securely through Razorpay with UPI, cards or net banking in INR, or international cards in USD.

Can I cancel?

Plans are prepaid for a month or a year and simply end if you don't renew. Nothing renews behind your back.
